## User preferences for this project

- Dark mode: video beside a single scrolling action-and-finding timeline. Findings travel with their actions; draw persistent connectors with short branches joining a shared rail per finding. Avoid crossing diagonal curves; keep background paths subdued and the current relationship clear. Use the heading “System of Record” and no right-column heading. Clicking an action or finding plays the corresponding clip directly. Draw connector lines into view, then reveal findings; respect reduced motion. Show a live playhead and progress on the actual current action, and scroll the shared timeline with playback. System of Record must display the exact AI actions. Human adjudications, including second-stop use, filter wetting and failed transfers, belong only in the findings on the right. Omit correction badges, source/audit explanations, linked-action counts, timestamp lists, or extra buttons. Keep the underlying AI record and human mapping intact privately. Keep the exact current AI action pinned at the top of the video, independently of findings; hide the overlay entirely in annotation gaps. Put the active finding with its full description below the scrubber, with no dismiss control. Retain the deduplicated error-note count at the top.
- Protocol stages are manually segmented from observed work, start expanded, and show durations plus error/observation/risk counts to the right of the step title. Hide protocol instruction prose. Start paused at the first Major error with its finding selected; preserve explicit shared timestamps, including zero. Step titles and scrubber chapters seek; chevrons only expand/collapse. Unknown stages have no invented time. Use independent Major and Minor multi-select filters at the top, initially Major only. Minor restores all other human notes, including second-stop, bubbles and filter concerns, with scientific uncertainty preserved rather than asserted as fact. Step counts, highlights and finding placement use the selected tiers and explicit ownership; context links cannot cause zero-count steps to display errors. Header filters show available major/minor error counts. Scrubber markers include all three categories, group nearby same-kind notes, and show a small colored chapter indicator for run-level omissions, never a long red band or invented point event.
- Keep the interface unbranded: no Transfyr logo or customer name. Access page shows only one password input, submitted with Enter. Remove download, copy-link, and logout/exit controls; attachment download requests are disabled. Expanding/collapsing a step anchors the timeline at that step header without seeking or jumping back to the selected action.
- Category colors are invariant: observations blue, errors red, risks yellow. Selection and playback retain the category color on actions, findings, connectors, and overlays.
- Vercel deployments must target the personal `joe-5572` scope. Never use the Transfyr team.
- This GitHub repo is public. Customer videos, review data, environment files and evidence-derived ingestion code stay out of Git.
- Human notes are the primary adjudication; keep AI action descriptions distinguishable.
