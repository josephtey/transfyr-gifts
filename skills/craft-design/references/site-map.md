## Index previews, not published essays

The index groups the following upcoming topics. Treat their brief descriptions as prompts for judgment, not researched prescriptions:

- Craft: perceived performance, gathering references, spending novelty sparingly, training taste, durable structure.
- Typography: size-dependent tracking, balanced wrapping, consistent icons, light-on-dark font rendering.
- Color: perceptual color spaces and layered shadows.
- Layout: generous hit targets, clipping reveals, directional scroll fades, continuous corner shapes.
- Motion: icon changes, press feedback, easing, stagger, interruptibility, shared layout, exit timing, and entrances from the trigger.
- Sound: quiet confirmation and layering short sounds.
- Data: continuous chart updates and smoothing that preserves the underlying values.
